## Four conservative tuition ceilings

These are ceilings under the stated published-rate planning assumptions, not exact bills or guaranteed future prices. A budget covering the ceiling can pass the tuition check. A smaller budget remains unknown because the actual bill can be lower. The existing two-sided estimates and Duke minimum-only bound keep their separate behavior.

All four ceilings use 2026-27 reference rates for the pinned Fall 2027 planning intake. They exclude future increases, fees, insurance, living costs, and additional/extended study. They never satisfy a guaranteed-final-price request. Illinois and CMU term-count ceilings apply only to the documented ordinary full-time scope; they do not carry into part-time or later-intake scenarios. Capped credit-based ceilings cover the ordinary required-credit scope, not unrestricted extra enrollment.
