The [2027–28 calendar](https://secfac.wisc.edu/academic-calendar/) lists September 2, 2027 instruction start, giving September 3, 2025 as the earliest qualifying date under that strict rule. Lower in-person ranges require program approval, ESLAT and any ESL course, so they remain unknown. A score still valid on today's assessment date can fail the future enrollment check.
- **UW Tacoma:** [Policy 3.2](https://grad.uw.edu/policies/3-2-graduate-school-english-language-proficiency-requirements/) measures two-year validity at application submission or the deferral petition. It is not replaced by a future-enrollment cutoff. The TOEFL and IELTS routes are now checked; qualifying scores below the recommended ELP level remain for AEP/fee review. DET program acceptance and exemptions are not inferred.
- **Cornell:** the CS page sets a **5.5 new-scale TOEFL speaking minimum**, higher than the general professional-master table's 4. Other new-scale section floors are writing 4, listening 3.5 and reading 4; IELTS 7 is accepted. The current department-specific legacy thresholds and exact application-deadline validity remain unconfirmed, so legacy TOEFL returns unknown and meeting a supported score floor still does not establish an English pass. The attempted fresh Graduate School capture returned 403 and was not imported.

No test-score conversion is performed. Missing dates, missing sources, conditional approval and later intake years remain unknown as appropriate. The stricter department policy takes precedence over a lower university-wide threshold.
