## Two additional cost calculations

**NYU Tandon:** the captured [official 2026-27 tuition PDF](https://www.nyu.edu/content/dam/nyu/bursar/documents/2026-2027%20Tuition%20and%20Fees.pdf), pages 2, 4 and 6, shows the graduate General rate of USD 2,626 per credit. Its footnote confirms per-credit charging. The separately sourced 30-credit MSCS requirement gives `2,626 × 30 = USD 78,780`. Undergraduate flat rates, Financial Engineering cohort rates, fees and living costs are not substituted into this calculation. Fall 2027 prices can change.

**Cornell Ithaca:** the academic-planning FAQ restricts current admission to two semesters, which is the selected one-academic-year track. The [engineering funding page](https://www.duffield.cornell.edu/meng/paying-for-your-meng-degree/) confirms MEng's Tier 1 category. The [university FY27 operating budget](https://dbp.cornell.edu/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/FY27-Operating-and-Capital-Budget-Plan.pdf), PDF page 21 / printed page 19, lists USD 73,946 in the 2026-27 Tier 1 annual column and includes MEng in the tier footnote. The selected track therefore has a `73,946 × 1 academic year = USD 73,946` **budget-plan-based estimate**. It is not a guaranteed final Bursar bill, a per-credit quote or a Fall 2027 price guarantee. Non-tuition fees, insurance, living costs, extra preparation and unconfirmed aid remain outside the calculation.

Both PDF tables were checked visually against their extracted rows and headers. Rates, time/credit units and assumptions are bound to the program fields and snapshots. Tests cover exact-budget/one-cent-under boundaries, broken credit evidence and the guaranteed-final-budget refusal. Seven computable estimates do not imply seven fully eligible or tier-supported recommendations.
