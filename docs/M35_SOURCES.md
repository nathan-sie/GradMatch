# M3.5 source review

Reviewed 2026-11-16. Current seed: 10 distinct programs, 30 official source records. Original M1 snapshots are retained byte-for-byte. Each new source has its actual HTTPS response SHA-256, retrieval timestamp, short excerpt or authored summary, field bindings and snapshot hash. Full raw pages/PDFs are private development material in ignored `work/m35_research/`. The application uses versioned snapshots; it does not fetch websites at runtime in this milestone.

## Exact-program admission evidence

The [official SCS comparison page](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/education/masters/programs-comparison) identifies its application and admitted columns as 2025. Its [linked JSON datasource](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/rs-datasources/data-source/scs-masters-programs-anyyear) supplies MCDS 481 applications / 145 offers and MSAII 526 / 58. Both the column-year page and exact-count response are separately captured and bound. These are program-level cohorts; the data does not establish a particular applicant's probability. MCDS's aggregate label cannot override its unknown eligibility checks.

Rubric conservative-v1.1 uses explicit engineering thresholds, <=15% Lottery and >15% to <=35% Reach. The labels are not classifications supplied by CMU and are not calibrated predictions. Rates alone cannot establish Target or Safer. This policy is shared by the baseline and later agents, before formal evaluation.

MSAII tuition: 4 × 32,400 + (6–12) × 650 = USD 133,500–137,400. Summer preparation is tuition even when the source calls it a course fee. MCDS standard timing: 3 × 32,400 = USD 97,200 before any selected additional courses. Internship and academic billing periods are distinguished. No assumed aid is deducted. Mandatory non-tuition fees, insurance and living costs are outside the tuition-only budget. Using 2026–27 rates for Fall 2027 is an explicit estimate; future price increases are unknown.

The [generic SCS reference](https://www.cmu.edu/sfs/tuition/graduate/scs.html) lists a different rate and warns that actual program tuition varies. The directly applicable departmental rate is used. Old FAQ dates and typical-score ranges are not promoted into current hard minima.
