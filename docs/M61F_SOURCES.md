## Minimum-only tuition

Duke's minimum is 3 × USD 34,940 = **USD 104,820 at published 2026–27 rates**. No verified maximum duration or total has been established. It is stored with a lower bound and no upper bound, with exact bindings to both the duration and rate sources. Extra enrollment, summer study, future increases, fees and living expenses may add cost.

For a published-rate budget, USD 100,000 fails the minimum check. Budgets of USD 104,820 or USD 140,000 remain unknown because a minimum cannot prove affordability. A request for a guaranteed future final bill remains unknown. Missing, changed or cross-program evidence cannot supply the bound. The seven existing full tuition estimates are counted separately.

## Capture provenance

`m61f_capture_receipts.json` preserves 13 transport receipts. Twelve substantive captures supply the imported sources. The `duke_rates` endpoint returned HTTP 200 with a 106-byte body and no extracted evidence; it was excluded. HTTP success alone is not evidence acceptance. Duke's substantive bulletin capture supplies the rate instead.

Each imported snapshot retains the response SHA-256, official URL, retrieval timestamp, locator, short exact excerpt, authored summary and verified field bindings. Optional raw responses are stored only under ignored local research files. Historical source objects, candidate data, homework results and secrets are preserved.
